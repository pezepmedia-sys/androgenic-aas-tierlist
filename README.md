# ANDROGENIC — AAS & SARM Tier List

A drag-and-drop tier list board of 35 anabolic-androgenic steroids and SARMs,
plus the "Getting Help" card.

Everything lives in a single self-contained `index.html` — all card art is
embedded, so there are no external assets and no build step to host it.

Card art is generated from each compound's 2D structure. The stripe colour is
the chemical family (blue testosterone-derived, red 19-nor, mint DHT-derived,
violet SARM, amber sold-as-a-SARM) and the corner glyph is the route
(syringe injectable, capsule oral).

Drag to rank, or tap a card then tap a tier on touch screens. Double-click a
card to remove it, drop your own image onto a card to replace its artwork.
Boards save to `localStorage`.
